Part 1: the lab

From scan to aligner, without leaving the building.

Most aligners are made in a factory far away. Mine are made down the corridor, so I check every set myself and refinements come back fast.

  1. Scan

    A 3D digital model of your teeth

  2. Plan

    Every movement designed by me

  3. Print

    A model for each stage

  4. Form & trim

    Clear trays shaped and polished

  5. Checked & sent

    Fit verified, then delivered
