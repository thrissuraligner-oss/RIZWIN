Orthodontist in Thrissur: clear aligners and braces at AVM Mall

Dr. Rizwin's orthodontic clinic is at AVM Mall, Civil Lane Road, Sakthan, Laloor, in the centre of Thrissur. Clear aligners, metal, self-ligating and lingual braces, children's orthodontics and second opinions.

In short

Dr. Rizwin is an orthodontist and clear aligner specialist practising at AVM Mall, Civil Lane Road, Sakthan, Laloor, Thrissur, Kerala 680003. He plans every aligner case himself, has the aligners made in his own in-house unit, and also offers braces and online consultations.

Thrissur is where the practice is based, so this is where your records are taken, your aligners are fitted and your reviews happen if you live in or near the city. The clinic is in the Sakthan and Laloor area of the city, a short auto-rickshaw ride from Thrissur railway station and the main bus stands.

Because the aligners are designed and made in Dr. Rizwin's own unit in Thrissur, a tray that needs adjusting, a lost aligner or a refinement can be handled locally rather than waiting on an outside lab. That is one of the practical advantages of choosing an orthodontist whose aligner production is in the same city.

Getting to the clinic

The clinic is at AVM Mall, Civil Lane Road, Sakthan, Laloor, Thrissur 680003. From Thrissur railway station or the KSRTC stand it is a short auto-rickshaw or taxi ride. If you are driving, search for “Dr.Rizwin, AVM Mall” in Google Maps.

Who visits the Thrissur clinic

Adults who want discreet clear aligners, teenagers, parents bringing a child for an early bite check (ideally around age 7), people whose teeth moved after old braces, and patients who want a second opinion on a plan from elsewhere. Patients from towns across Thrissur district, such as Guruvayur, Kunnamkulam, Chalakudy, Irinjalakuda, Kodungallur and Wadakkanchery, can reach the clinic easily.

Treatments available in Thrissur

Clear aligners (Dr. Rizwin's main focus), metal braces, self-ligating braces, lingual braces hidden behind the teeth, palatal expanders, myofunctional and functional appliances for growing children, retainers, adult interdisciplinary care, and second opinions. Every option is chosen by diagnosis, not by trend.

How treatment works at the clinic

  1. 1

    Online consultation

    A 30-minute video call with Dr. Rizwin. You send smile photos first, so the call is about your teeth, not paperwork. No travel needed.

  2. 2

    Records

    If treatment is advised, your X-rays and 3D scan are arranged at the Thrissur clinic or at a partner clinic nearer to you.

  3. 3

    3D plan review

    Your tooth movement plan, treatment time and written fee are explained online. You approve only when you are happy.

  4. 4

    Aligners delivered

    Aligners are made in Dr. Rizwin's in-house aligner unit and fitted at the clinic, with attachments placed if your plan needs them.

  5. 5

    Reviews

    Check-ins mix online reviews with in-person visits. Aligner patients usually need far fewer clinic visits than braces patients.

Questions from Thrissur patients

Where is Dr. Rizwin's clinic in Thrissur?

At AVM Mall, Civil Lane Road, Sakthan, Laloor, Thrissur, Kerala 680003. Book on WhatsApp through the consultation form, or email thrissuraligner@gmail.com.

Do I need a referral from my dentist?

No. You can book directly. If you already have X-rays or scans from your dentist, bring them along; they may save time.

Can I see Dr. Rizwin online before visiting?

Yes. A 30-minute online video consultation lets you discuss your concern first. If treatment is advised, records are then taken at the clinic.

Does Dr. Rizwin treat children in Thrissur?

Yes. A first orthodontic check around age 7 helps spot crossbites, crowding and habits early, when growth can still help.

How soon can I start aligners after the consultation?

After your records and 3D plan are approved, aligners are made in the in-house unit, usually within weeks. The exact time depends on your case.
