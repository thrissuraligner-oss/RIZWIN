In short

Patients from Kozhikode (Calicut) can consult Dr. Rizwin online first, then travel to Thrissur for records and aligner fitting. The rail distance is about 118 km; the fastest trains take around 1.5 hours and most take 2 to 3 hours, with many trains each day.

Kozhikode is one of the larger cities Dr. Rizwin's patients travel from. With frequent trains on the Kozhikode–Shoranur–Thrissur line, a single appointment fits into a day, and aligner treatment keeps the number of trips low because most check-ins can happen online.

This page explains how the visits work, how long the journey takes, and what Calicut patients usually ask before deciding.

How treatment works from Kozhikode

Start with an online video consultation. If aligners or braces are advised, your records are taken in Thrissur or arranged at a partner clinic nearer to you. You review the 3D plan online, then come to Thrissur for fitting. After that, online check-ins replace many routine visits.

Braces or aligners if you live in Calicut?

Distance matters more with braces, which need regular adjustment visits. With aligners, you change trays at home on schedule, so fewer trips are needed. That said, your bite decides the appliance; if braces are the better choice for your teeth, Dr. Rizwin will say so and plan visits around your travel.

Who travels from Kozhikode

Adults who want a specialist-planned aligner treatment, families seeking a second opinion for a teenager, and patients from the wider Kozhikode district, including Koyilandy, Feroke and Ramanattukara, who prefer to plan treatment with one orthodontist from start to finish.

Your visit plan from Kozhikode

  1. 1

    Online consultation

    A 30-minute video call with Dr. Rizwin. You send smile photos first, so the call is about your teeth, not paperwork. No travel needed.

  2. 2

    Records

    If treatment is advised, your X-rays and 3D scan are arranged at the Thrissur clinic or at a partner clinic nearer to you.

  3. 3

    3D plan review

    Your tooth movement plan, treatment time and written fee are explained online. You approve only when you are happy.

  4. 4

    Aligners delivered

    Aligners are made in Dr. Rizwin's in-house aligner unit and fitted at the clinic, with attachments placed if your plan needs them.

  5. 5

    Reviews

    Check-ins mix online reviews with in-person visits. Aligner patients usually need far fewer clinic visits than braces patients.

Questions from Kozhikode patients

How many times will I need to travel from Kozhikode?

It depends on your case. Typically there is one visit for records and one for fitting, then occasional in-person reviews. Your plan states the expected visits before you start.

Can my records be taken in Kozhikode?

Where possible, records are arranged at a partner clinic nearer to you. Ask during your online consultation.

What if I need help between visits?

Message the clinic on WhatsApp. Many questions, such as a loose attachment or a tight tray, can be handled with photos or a quick video call.

Is Calicut too far for braces?

Not necessarily, but braces need regular adjustments, so plan for more trips. If aligners suit your bite, they usually need fewer visits.

Do you offer weekend appointments?

Ask when you book. We try to schedule travelling patients at times that suit train timings.
