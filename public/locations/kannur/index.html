Aligner treatment for patients from Kannur

Kannur is the farthest of the North Kerala towns we serve: about 208 km from Thrissur by rail. Treatment is planned so that most of your care happens online.

Book a consultationHow visits work

In short

Patients from Kannur can consult Dr. Rizwin online first and travel to Thrissur only for the visits that need the clinic, such as records and aligner fitting. The rail distance is about 208 km; the fastest train takes around 2.5 hours and most take 3.5 to 4.5 hours.

Travelling from Kannur takes planning, and it is fair to ask whether a specialist in Thrissur is worth the trip compared with a clinic nearer home. For aligner treatment, the number of trips can be kept low because trays are changed at home and many check-ins happen by video.

This page sets out the journey, the visits and the questions Kannur patients ask most, so you can decide with real information.

How treatment works from Kannur

Your first step is an online video consultation. If treatment is advised, records are arranged at the Thrissur clinic or at a partner clinic nearer to you. You review your 3D plan online, then come to Thrissur for fitting. After that, online check-ins handle most routine follow-up.

Planning the trip

Faster trains make a same-day return possible for many patients. Tell us you are travelling from Kannur when you book; we will time your appointment around train schedules and group steps where possible.

Who travels from Kannur

Patients from Kannur, Thalassery, Payyanur and Taliparamba who want one orthodontist to plan and supervise their aligners, including patients home from the Gulf who want treatment planned before or during a visit.

Your visit plan from Kannur

  1. 1

    Online consultation

    A 30-minute video call with Dr. Rizwin. You send smile photos first, so the call is about your teeth, not paperwork. No travel needed.

  2. 2

    Records

    If treatment is advised, your X-rays and 3D scan are arranged at the Thrissur clinic or at a partner clinic nearer to you.

  3. 3

    3D plan review

    Your tooth movement plan, treatment time and written fee are explained online. You approve only when you are happy.

  4. 4

    Aligners delivered

    Aligners are made in Dr. Rizwin's in-house aligner unit and fitted at the clinic, with attachments placed if your plan needs them.

  5. 5

    Reviews

    Check-ins mix online reviews with in-person visits. Aligner patients usually need far fewer clinic visits than braces patients.

Questions from Kannur patients

Is it realistic to do aligner treatment in Thrissur from Kannur?

For many patients, yes. Aligner treatment needs few in-person visits, and the fastest trains take about 2.5 hours. Your consultation will tell you how many visits your case needs.

I live in the Gulf and visit Kannur. Can I still get treatment?

Often, yes. Consult online from abroad, plan before you fly, and do your scan and fitting during your visit. Monitoring continues online.

Should I choose a closer orthodontist instead?

That is your decision. Choose the orthodontist whose diagnosis and follow-up you trust most. The FAQ covers what to ask any clinic.

What happens if an aligner cracks?

Message the clinic on WhatsApp with a photo. You will usually move to the next aligner or go back one while a replacement is made in the in-house unit.

Do Kannur patients pay more?

No. The fee depends on your treatment, not where you live, and you get it in writing after your 3D plan.
