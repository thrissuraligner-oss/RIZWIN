Aligner treatment for patients from Malappuram

Thrissur is about 80 km from Malappuram town by road. Patients from Malappuram district reach the clinic by road or through the railway stations at Tirur, Kuttippuram or Angadipuram.

In short

Malappuram patients can begin with an online consultation with Dr. Rizwin and come to his Thrissur clinic for records and fitting. Malappuram town is about 80 km from Thrissur by road, usually a 2 to 3 hour journey; rail travellers use stations such as Tirur or Kuttippuram on the main line.

Malappuram district is large, so the easiest route to Thrissur depends on where you live. Patients near the coast often take a train from Tirur or Kuttippuram, while those around Malappuram town, Manjeri or Perinthalmanna usually travel by road.

Whichever route you take, the plan is the same: do as much as possible online and keep in-person visits for the steps that need them.

How treatment works from Malappuram

Send your smile photos and book a video consultation. If treatment is advised, records are taken in Thrissur or arranged nearer to you. You see your 3D plan online and come to Thrissur for fitting. Online check-ins cover much of the follow-up, with in-person reviews when the plan calls for them.

Families from Malappuram

Many enquiries from Malappuram are about children: crowded teeth, a narrow upper jaw or habits like thumb-sucking and tongue thrust. An early check, ideally around age 7, can show whether treatment is needed now or simply monitoring. See child orthodontics and myofunctional therapy.

Who travels from Malappuram

Patients from Malappuram town, Manjeri, Perinthalmanna, Tirur, Kottakkal and Ponnani who want one orthodontist to plan and follow their treatment from start to finish.

Your visit plan from Malappuram

  1. 1

    Online consultation

    A 30-minute video call with Dr. Rizwin. You send smile photos first, so the call is about your teeth, not paperwork. No travel needed.

  2. 2

    Records

    If treatment is advised, your X-rays and 3D scan are arranged at the Thrissur clinic or at a partner clinic nearer to you.

  3. 3

    3D plan review

    Your tooth movement plan, treatment time and written fee are explained online. You approve only when you are happy.

  4. 4

    Aligners delivered

    Aligners are made in Dr. Rizwin's in-house aligner unit and fitted at the clinic, with attachments placed if your plan needs them.

  5. 5

    Reviews

    Check-ins mix online reviews with in-person visits. Aligner patients usually need far fewer clinic visits than braces patients.

Questions from Malappuram patients

Which railway station is best from Malappuram?

It depends on where you live. Tirur and Kuttippuram are on the main line to Thrissur; Angadipuram is on the Nilambur branch. Many patients from Malappuram town prefer to come by road.

Can I get aligners for my child from Malappuram?

Yes, if aligners suit your child's teeth and they can wear them consistently. Some children need expanders or functional appliances first; the consultation decides.

How many trips will treatment need?

Usually a records visit and a fitting visit, then occasional reviews. Your plan sets this out before you start.

Can I message the clinic during treatment?

Yes. WhatsApp is the quickest way to ask about a tight tray, a lost aligner or a loose attachment.

Is the consultation fee adjusted if I start treatment?

The consultation fee is adjusted against treatment if you go ahead.
