Aligner treatment for patients from Shoranur

Shoranur Junction is about 32 km from Thrissur by rail, with trains taking roughly 30 minutes to an hour. Of the towns we serve outside Thrissur, it is the closest.

In short

Patients from Shoranur can reach Dr. Rizwin's Thrissur clinic in about 30 minutes to an hour by train from Shoranur Junction, a rail distance of roughly 32 km. That makes in-person reviews easy, though you can still start with an online consultation.

Because Shoranur Junction is a major rail hub with frequent trains to Thrissur, patients from Shoranur and nearby Ottapalam, Cheruthuruthy and Pattambi can attend appointments almost as easily as people living in Thrissur itself.

Short travel time also gives you more choice: both aligners and braces are practical, because adjustment visits for braces are not a long trip.

How treatment works from Shoranur

You can come straight to the clinic for your consultation or start online. Records, the 3D plan review and aligner fitting follow. Because the trip is short, reviews can be in person whenever that suits you.

Aligners or braces from Shoranur

With an easy train ride, distance does not limit your choice. Aligners suit many adults who want discreet treatment; braces may be better for some complex bites. Read aligners vs braces for a fair comparison.

Who travels from Shoranur

Patients from Shoranur, Ottapalam, Cheruthuruthy, Pattambi and Vaniyamkulam who prefer a specialist orthodontist in Thrissur over a long trip to a larger city.

Your visit plan from Shoranur

  1. 1

    Online consultation

    A 30-minute video call with Dr. Rizwin. You send smile photos first, so the call is about your teeth, not paperwork. No travel needed.

  2. 2

    Records

    If treatment is advised, your X-rays and 3D scan are arranged at the Thrissur clinic or at a partner clinic nearer to you.

  3. 3

    3D plan review

    Your tooth movement plan, treatment time and written fee are explained online. You approve only when you are happy.

  4. 4

    Aligners delivered

    Aligners are made in Dr. Rizwin's in-house aligner unit and fitted at the clinic, with attachments placed if your plan needs them.

  5. 5

    Reviews

    Check-ins mix online reviews with in-person visits. Aligner patients usually need far fewer clinic visits than braces patients.

Questions from Shoranur patients

How long is the train from Shoranur to Thrissur?

About 30 minutes on the fastest trains, and up to about an hour on slower ones.

Can I attend reviews in person?

Yes. With a short journey, in-person reviews are easy to fit in, and online check-ins are available when they are more convenient.

Is braces treatment practical from Shoranur?

Yes. Regular braces adjustments are manageable when the clinic is under an hour away.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Any recent X-rays, records of previous orthodontic treatment, and a list of questions. Your phone with clear smile photos helps too.

How do I book?

Use the consultation form; it sends your details and photos to the clinic on WhatsApp.
