Orthodontic treatment in Thrissur: aligners, braces and more

Aligners are my specialty, but your treatment is chosen for one reason only: it’s what your smile truly needs.

By Dr. Rizwin, orthodontist and clear aligner specialist, ThrissurUpdated 28 October 2026

Every smile has its own path. I’ll find the right one for yours.

Clear aligners

Nearly invisible, removable trays that move teeth step by step, for crowding, spacing, forward teeth, many bite problems and relapse after braces. Every plan is designed by me and made in my own lab.

Metal braces

Strong, reliable and time-tested. Precise, around-the-clock control for complex movements and severe crowding, and still the gold standard for many cases.

Self-ligating braces

Modern braces with built-in clips instead of elastic ties: less friction, fewer adjustments, easier cleaning and a more comfortable journey.

Lingual braces

Braces bonded behind the teeth, completely hidden from view. The full power of braces with no one able to tell.

Myofunctional therapy

Myofunctional and functional appliances that correct the habits behind crooked teeth (mouth breathing, tongue thrust, thumb-sucking) and guide growing jaws while growth can still help.

Expanders

Palatal expanders widen a narrow upper jaw and create room for crowded teeth. Best started early: a first check around age 7 helps prevent bigger problems later.

Adult & interdisciplinary care

Discreet adult treatment, coordinated with implants, gum care or smile design when needed.

Retainers

Fixed and removable retainers that protect your new smile, so the result you worked for stays with you for life.

Second opinions

Unsure about treatment started elsewhere, or a plan you’ve been given? Get an honest specialist review, in person or online.

Child orthodontics

Guiding jaw growth and early bite problems at the right age.
