Palatal expanders in Thrissur

Room for every tooth: widening a narrow upper jaw so the teeth can line up and the bite can fit.

Book an online consultationVisit the Thrissur clinic

By Dr. Rizwin, orthodontist and clear aligner specialist, ThrissurUpdated 28 October 2026

Expanders widen a narrow upper jaw, so the teeth have room to line up and the upper and lower teeth fit together properly.

Why widen the jaw?

A narrow upper jaw can cause crowding, crossbites, and sometimes breathing problems. Widening it treats the cause instead of squeezing teeth into too little space.

What it feels like

A feeling of pressure around the teeth and nose for a few days. A small gap may appear between the front teeth during expansion; it’s a good sign that it’s working, and it closes on its own.

Types of expanders

  • Rapid palatal expander (RPE / Hyrax)A fixed appliance attached to the back teeth, with a small screw in the middle of the palate. It’s turned a little each day to widen the upper jaw. Best for children and young teens.
  • MARPEMini-implant assisted rapid palatal expansion. Anchored to the palate with tiny mini-implants, it can widen the jaw in older teens and some adults, often avoiding surgery.
  • Quad helixA spring-wire expander for gentle widening, also used to correct crossbites and rotated back teeth.
  • Removable expansion platesPlates with an expansion screw, used for mild widening in younger children.

Best suited for

Narrow upper jaws, crossbites, and crowding caused by lack of space, especially in children and teens.

Questions

Does a palatal expander hurt?

Expect a feeling of pressure around the teeth and nose for a few days.

Can adults have their upper jaw widened?

MARPE, anchored with tiny mini-implants, can widen the jaw in older teens and some adults, often avoiding surgery.
