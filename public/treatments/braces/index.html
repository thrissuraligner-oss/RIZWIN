Braces in Thrissur: the classical pathway

Fixed appliances that guide teeth with a wire or arms bonded to the teeth. Long established, versatile and precise, including for complex cases.

How braces work

Braces are brackets bonded to the teeth and joined by a wire. The shape and springiness of the wire gently guide each tooth. The wire is changed through stages, from soft and flexible to firm and precise, so teeth are levelled, moved and finished in a controlled sequence.

Most common

Metal braces

The familiar stainless-steel system with elastic ties.

 Built-in clip

Self-ligating braces

A clip replaces the elastic ties. See what changes in practice.

 Hidden

Lingual braces and Brava

Bonded behind the teeth, invisible from the front.

When braces are often the better choice

  • Complex tooth movements: rotations, torque, vertical control
  • Bite problems that need elastics or added appliances
  • Cases combined with jaw surgery or extractions
  • People who prefer not to manage removable trays
