Lingual braces and Brava in Thrissur

Fixed braces bonded behind the teeth, hidden from view, with the control of a fixed appliance.

Ask about lingual braces

Dr. Rizwin Aligner Orthodontist, ThrissurPending clinical review by Dr. RizwinUpdated 29 September 2026

Lingual braces are bonded to the inside (tongue side) of your teeth, so they’re completely hidden when you smile.

How they look

From the front, you can’t see them at all. Only you will know they’re there.

How they work

The brackets are often custom-made for the inner surfaces of your teeth. A wire works just like regular braces, only from behind, giving full control over every movement.

Who chooses them

Professionals, performers, brides and grooms, and anyone who wants the power of fixed braces with complete invisibility.

Things to know

Your tongue will feel them at first, and speech may change slightly for a week or two. Cleaning needs extra care. They usually cost more than braces on the front of the teeth.

Best suited for

Adults and teens who need fixed braces but don’t want anyone to see them.

What is lingual orthodontics?

Lingual braces are fixed braces bonded to the tongue side (the back) of the teeth, so they are hidden from view. Because they are fixed, they work all day and do not depend on you remembering to wear anything.

What is Brava?

Brava, made by Brius Technologies, is one lingual system. According to its manufacturer, each tooth carries an individually designed arm made from a shape-memory nickel-titanium alloy, instead of a single archwire linking every bracket, and the treatment plan is prepared digitally for each patient.

Those design claims come from the manufacturer. We rely on published, peer-reviewed evidence when advising you, and we will say plainly where evidence is limited.

Points to consider with any lingual appliance

  • Hidden from view, which many adults and teenagers value
  • Your tongue needs a few days to adapt, and speech can feel slightly different at first
  • Cleaning behind the teeth takes care and technique
  • Placement is more technique-sensitive and needs specific training
  • Not every bite or tooth shape is suitable, so assessment comes first
  • Fees are usually higher than for conventional braces because of the custom work

Lingual braces vs aligners. Both are discreet. Lingual braces are fixed and can handle complex movements, while aligners are removable and suit many mild to moderate cases. The choice depends on your teeth, your bite and how you want to live during treatment.
