Child orthodontics and dentofacial orthopedics

Some bite problems begin in the jaws, and growth can be guided while a child is still growing.

Dr. Rizwin Aligner Orthodontist, ThrissurPending clinical review by Dr. RizwinUpdated 29 September 2026

What is dentofacial orthopedics?

Dentofacial orthopedics is the part of orthodontics that guides jaw growth and the relationship between the upper and lower jaws. It is most effective while a child is still growing.

Many orthodontic bodies suggest a first check-up around age 7. That does not mean starting treatment then. It means having a specialist check whether anything needs early attention, or simply monitoring.

Early assessment can help with

  • Upper and lower jaws growing out of step
  • Crossbites and narrow arches
  • Habits such as prolonged thumb-sucking
  • Severely crowded or protruding front teeth
  • Teeth erupting in the wrong place

What to expect

A gentle examination, photographs and, where indicated, X-rays. Many children need only monitoring. If treatment is advised, we explain the options to parent and child in plain language.
