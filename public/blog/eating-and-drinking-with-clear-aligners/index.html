Eating and drinking with clear aligners

No food is banned with aligners, but the routine around meals matters. A practical guide, including Kerala favourites.

Dr. Rizwin Aligner Orthodontist, ThrissurPending clinical review by Dr. RizwinUpdated 30 October 2026

Quick answer

With clear aligners you can eat almost anything, because you take the aligners out to eat. Remove them for every meal and for any drink except plain water, then rinse or brush before putting them back. This prevents staining, warping and food trapped against your teeth.

The three rules

  1. Out to eat: remove aligners for every meal and snack.
  2. Only plain water with aligners in: no tea, coffee, juice, milk or soft drinks.
  3. Clean before replacing: brush, or at least rinse well, so food and sugar are not sealed against your teeth.

Tea, coffee and Kerala food

Chai and coffee are fine with aligners out. Hot drinks can warp the plastic and sugar trapped under the tray raises the risk of decay, so drink them with aligners removed. Turmeric-rich curries and coloured foods can stain aligners, which is another reason to eat with them out.

Sipping sweet tea through the day is the commonest reason for low wear time; try grouping it with meals.

Keeping aligners clean and clear

  • Rinse aligners each time you remove them.
  • Brush them gently with a soft brush and cool water; avoid toothpaste, which can scratch.
  • Never use hot water.
  • Keep them in their case when out of your mouth.

A simple routine for each meal

  1. Remove aligners and put them in their case, never in a napkin.
  2. Eat your meal and drink what you like.
  3. Brush your teeth, or rinse well with water if you can't brush.
  4. Rinse the aligners and put them back in.

The whole routine usually takes a few extra minutes and keeps teeth and aligners clean.

Kerala foods and aligners

No Kerala food is off limits, because aligners come out to eat. The things to watch are what happens around the meal: turmeric and coloured curries can stain if aligners go back in without rinsing, sweet tea and payasam leave sugar against the teeth, and frequent snacking cuts your wear time.

Biryani at a wedding, sadya on Onam or porotta at a thattukada are all fine: take the aligners out, enjoy the meal, clean up and put them back.

Why sugar under aligners is a problem

An aligner holds saliva away from the teeth. If sugary or acidic drinks are trapped underneath, they stay in contact with the enamel for longer, raising the risk of decay. That is why only plain water is recommended with aligners in.

Eating out, travel and functions

Carry your case, a travel toothbrush and a small bottle of water. At weddings and functions, take aligners out for the meal and put them back as soon as practical. See how many hours a day to wear aligners.

Frequently asked questions

Can I chew gum with aligners?

No. Remove aligners first, and prefer sugar-free gum.

Can I drink tea with aligners in?

It is best not to. Remove them for tea and coffee.

Do I have to brush after every meal?

Ideally yes; if you can't, rinse your mouth and aligners well before replacing them.

Will aligners change what I can eat?

No food is banned, because aligners come out to eat.

Can I smoke with aligners in?

No. Smoking stains aligners and is harmful to your teeth and gums.
