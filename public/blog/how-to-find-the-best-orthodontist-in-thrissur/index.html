What is an orthodontist, and how is it different from a dentist?

An orthodontist is a dentist who has completed an additional three-year postgraduate degree, the MDS in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics, focused on how teeth move and how jaws grow. A general dentist may offer aligners or braces, but an orthodontist's whole training is in diagnosing and correcting bites.

In India, specialists are registered with their State Dental Council, and many orthodontists are members of the Indian Orthodontic Society. You can simply ask any clinic in Thrissur: “Who will plan my treatment, and what is their orthodontic qualification?” A good clinic answers this directly.

The 7-point checklist for choosing an orthodontist in Thrissur

  1. Specialist qualification. MDS in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics, and the name of the doctor who will actually plan and supervise your case.
  2. Proper diagnosis before a quote. Photos, X-rays (an OPG, and a cephalogram when the bite or jaws are involved), and a 3D scan or models. A price given after a quick look is a guess.
  3. Options, not a single product. Aligners, metal braces, self-ligating braces, lingual braces or growth-guidance appliances each suit different problems. An orthodontist who offers several can recommend honestly.
  4. A plan you can see. For aligners, a 3D simulation of the tooth movement. For braces, a clear explanation of stages and expected duration.
  5. A written, all-inclusive fee. Ask whether attachments, refinements, review visits, breakages and retainers are included.
  6. Retention plan. Teeth tend to move back without retainers.[1] Ask what retainers you get, for how long, and the cost of replacements.
  7. Follow-up you can manage. How often are visits, can some reviews be online, and who do you contact if something breaks?

Red flags to watch for

  • Treatment started without X-rays or a proper examination.
  • A fixed “package price” offered before anyone has looked at your bite.
  • Promises of certain results or unusually fast treatment for a complex problem.
  • No clarity about who plans the aligners: a specialist, a general dentist, or software alone.
  • No mention of retainers.

None of these automatically mean poor care, but each deserves a clear answer before you commit.

Questions to ask at your first orthodontic consultation

  • What exactly is wrong with my bite, and what happens if I do nothing?
  • Which options suit my case, and why do you recommend this one?
  • How long will treatment take, and how many visits will I need?
  • What is included in the fee, and what could cost extra?
  • What happens if my teeth don't track as planned?
  • How long will I need retainers?

How much does orthodontic treatment cost in Thrissur?

Fees in Thrissur vary with the problem, the appliance and what is included. Prices published by Kerala clinics put clear aligners from roughly ₹40,000 for simple cases to well over ₹2,00,000 for complex treatment with premium brands. Compare all-inclusive written fees, not headline prices.

Practical things to check at a Thrissur clinic

  • Getting there: how easy the clinic is to reach from your home, college or office, by bus, auto or car.
  • Appointment times: whether review times fit around work or school.
  • Records: whether X-rays and 3D scans are done at the clinic or arranged elsewhere.
  • Problems between visits: who to contact about a broken bracket, a loose wire or a lost aligner, and how quickly it is handled.
  • Continuity: whether the same orthodontist sees you at each review.

Frequently asked questions

How do I know if an orthodontist in Thrissur is a specialist?

Ask for the doctor's qualification. An orthodontic specialist in India holds an MDS in Orthodontics and Dentofacial Orthopaedics and is registered with the State Dental Council.

Do I need a referral to see an orthodontist?

No. You can book directly with an orthodontist without a referral from a general dentist.

Are online reviews a good way to choose?

They show patient experience and communication, but not clinical quality. Use them alongside qualification, diagnosis and a clear written plan.

What age should a child first see an orthodontist?

Around age 7 is a common recommendation for a first check, so problems with jaw growth or crossbites can be caught early.

Can I get a second opinion in Thrissur?

Yes, and it is sensible for expensive or complex treatment. A second opinion reviews your records and the proposed plan.
