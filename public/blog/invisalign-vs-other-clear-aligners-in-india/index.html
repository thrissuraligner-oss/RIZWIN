Invisalign vs other clear aligners in India: what really differs

Brand names dominate aligner advertising. Here is what actually differs between systems, and what doesn't.

Dr. Rizwin Aligner Orthodontist, ThrissurPending clinical review by Dr. RizwinUpdated 30 October 2026

Quick answer

Invisalign, other international brands, Indian aligner brands and in-house aligners all straighten teeth with a sequence of custom clear trays. They differ in plastic, software, manufacturing and price, but the biggest influence on your result is the orthodontist who diagnoses your case, edits the plan and supervises treatment.

What all aligner systems share

Every system scans your teeth, creates a digital plan of small movements, and makes a series of trays, each worn for one to two weeks. Attachments and IPR are used across systems. The principle and the biology of tooth movement are the same.

Where they differ

Published prices in India put Invisalign treatment around ₹1,10,000 to ₹2,50,000 or more, with Indian brands often from about ₹40,000. See aligner cost in Kerala.

Why the clinician matters more than the logo

Even with leading systems, studies show only part of each planned movement is achieved in one round, with rotations among the hardest.[1][2] The orthodontist's plan edits, attachment choices, staging and monitoring are what close that gap.

Questions to ask about any aligner system

  • Who designs and edits my plan, and can I see the 3D simulation?
  • What plastic is used and how often are trays changed?
  • How quickly can a lost or broken aligner be replaced?
  • Are refinements included, and how many?
  • What retainers are included at the end?

Does the aligner material matter?

Material affects how the aligner holds its force, how clear it stays and how it feels. Differences exist between plastics, but research on aligner accuracy points to planning, attachments and staging as bigger influences on the final result than any one material.

Dr. Rizwin's postgraduate thesis compared different clear aligner materials.

When a premium brand may make sense

If you want a specific system's features, such as a particular teen programme or software, or you are moving abroad and want continuity with a widely available brand, a premium system can be a sensible choice. Discuss it at your consultation; the orthodontist should explain the trade-offs, not just quote a price.

Frequently asked questions

Is Invisalign better than other aligners?

It is a well-established system, but a well-planned case with another quality system can achieve similar results. The orthodontist matters most.

Are in-house aligners safe?

Yes, when made with medical-grade materials under proper supervision and planned by a qualified clinician.

Why is Invisalign more expensive?

Brand, imported manufacturing and software licensing all add to the cost.

Can I switch aligner brands mid-treatment?

It is possible but needs a new scan and plan. Discuss it with your orthodontist.

Which aligner brand is best for me?

Ask your orthodontist after diagnosis; the right choice depends on your case and budget.
