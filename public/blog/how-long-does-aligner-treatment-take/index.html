How long does clear aligner treatment take?

A straight answer, the typical timelines, and the factors that make treatment faster or slower.

Dr. Rizwin Aligner Orthodontist, ThrissurPending clinical review by Dr. RizwinUpdated 30 October 2026

Quick answer

Clear aligner treatment typically takes about 4 to 8 months for mild cases, 8 to 14 months for moderate cases and 12 to 24 months for complex cases. The biggest factors are how far teeth must move, the type of movement, and whether you wear the aligners 20 to 22 hours a day.

Typical aligner treatment times

CaseExamplesTypical duration
MildSmall gaps, slight crowding, minor relapse after braces4–8 months
ModerateNoticeable crowding or spacing, forward teeth, mild bite issues8–14 months
ComplexSevere crowding, deep or open bites, combined treatment12–24 months

These are the ranges used at Dr. Rizwin's practice for planning. Your own 3D plan gives your estimate, and retainers follow the active phase.

What makes aligner treatment faster or slower?

The amount and type of tooth movement, how consistently you wear the aligners, whether refinements are needed, and missed reviews or lost trays all change the timeline. Wearing aligners 20 to 22 hours a day is the factor you control most.

  • Amount of movement: small changes take less time than large ones.
  • Type of movement: tipping and levelling are more predictable than rotating rounded teeth or extruding teeth.[1]
  • Wear time: aligners only work when worn. Less than 20 to 22 hours a day slows or stalls progress.
  • Refinements: some cases need a further set of trays to fine-tune the result. Research shows not every planned movement is fully achieved in one round.[2]
  • Missed reviews or lost trays: each delay adds time.

Are aligners faster than braces?

In mild to moderate cases, research suggests aligners can be somewhat faster. A meta-analysis found shorter treatment time and chair time with aligners in mild to moderate cases.[3] One study found aligner patients finished about 5.7 months sooner, although braces were more likely to achieve a “great improvement”.[4]

For complex bites, braces may be more efficient. The right choice depends on your diagnosis; see aligners vs braces.

How often do you change aligners?

Most people change to a new aligner every one to two weeks, as set in their treatment plan. Changing early does not make treatment faster; teeth need time to follow each tray, and rushing can stop the aligners fitting properly.

Usually every 1 to 2 weeks, as set in your plan. Changing early does not speed things up: teeth need time to follow each tray, and rushing can cause the aligners to stop fitting.

What happens after the last aligner?

After the last aligner, retainers are fitted to hold the result, because teeth tend to drift back without them. Retention is usually full-time at first and then at night for the long term, as your orthodontist advises.

Retainers keep the result. Without retention, teeth tend to drift back towards where they started.[5] Read why retainers matter.

Frequently asked questions

Can aligners straighten teeth in 3 months?

Very mild cases can sometimes finish quickly, but most treatment takes longer. Be cautious of promises without an examination.

Does wearing aligners longer each day make treatment faster?

Wearing them 20 to 22 hours a day keeps you on schedule. Wearing them less slows treatment; wearing them more than prescribed does not speed it up.

How long is each aligner worn?

Usually 1 to 2 weeks, as set in your plan.

Will I need refinements?

Many patients need a short refinement phase to perfect the result. A good fee includes this.

How long do I wear retainers?

Usually long-term, often full-time at first and then at night. Your orthodontist will advise.
