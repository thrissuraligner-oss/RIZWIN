What happens at your first orthodontic consultation?

Knowing what to expect helps you ask better questions and compare clinics fairly.

Dr. Rizwin Aligner Orthodontist, ThrissurPending clinical review by Dr. RizwinUpdated 30 October 2026

Quick answer

At a first orthodontic consultation, the orthodontist asks about your concerns, examines your teeth, bite, gums and jaw, and explains whether you need treatment and which options suit you. If you proceed, records such as photos, X-rays and a 3D scan follow, then a written plan and fee.

Before the visit

  • Note what bothers you about your teeth or bite.
  • Bring recent X-rays and records of any earlier orthodontic treatment.
  • For an online consultation, take clear smile photos in daylight. Dr. Rizwin's photo guide shows the 8 views.

During the consultation

  1. Your concerns and history: what you want changed, medical history, habits.
  2. Examination: teeth, bite, gums, jaw joint and face.
  3. Options: aligners, braces or other appliances, and what happens without treatment.
  4. Next steps: records, approximate duration and how the fee is set.

Records and the 3D plan

If you go ahead, records are taken: photos, X-rays (such as an OPG and, when needed, a cephalogram) and a 3D intraoral scan. For aligners, the orthodontist then designs a 3D plan showing how your teeth will move, which you review before approving.

What to bring

  • Recent X-rays and dental records.
  • Records or retainers from any previous orthodontic treatment.
  • A list of medicines and medical conditions.
  • Your questions, written down.

Questions worth asking

  • What exactly is wrong, and what happens if I wait?
  • What are my options, and which do you recommend?
  • How long will it take and how many visits will I need?
  • What does the fee include?
  • What is the retention plan?

See how to choose an orthodontist for a fuller checklist.

After the consultation

You should leave knowing whether treatment is needed, the realistic options, a rough timeline and how the fee will be set. There should be no pressure to start that day. Once records are taken, you receive a written plan and fee to approve in your own time.

Online vs in-person first consultation

An online consultation is a convenient first step if you live outside Thrissur or abroad: you get an honest opinion from photos and a video call. A full diagnosis still needs records, taken at the clinic or a partner clinic.

Frequently asked questions

How long is a first consultation?

Dr. Rizwin's online consultation is a dedicated 30-minute video call.

Will I get a price at the first visit?

You will understand how the fee is set; the written fee follows your records and plan.

Do I have to start treatment after the consultation?

No. Take your time and ask questions.

Can I bring a family member?

Yes, especially for children and teenagers.

Is the consultation fee adjusted if I start treatment?

At Dr. Rizwin's practice, yes.

This article is general information, not a diagnosis. Individual treatment needs differ; consult an orthodontist about your own teeth.
