How many hours a day should you wear clear aligners?

The single biggest thing you control in aligner treatment is wear time. Here is how to get it right without disrupting your day.

Dr. Rizwin Aligner Orthodontist, ThrissurPending clinical review by Dr. RizwinUpdated 30 October 2026

Quick answer

Clear aligners should be worn 20 to 22 hours a day, removed only to eat, drink anything other than plain water, and clean your teeth. Teeth move only while the aligner is applying pressure, so wearing them less slows treatment, and can make the next aligner fit poorly.

Why 20 to 22 hours?

Aligners move teeth with gentle, continuous pressure. When the tray is out for long periods, that pressure stops and teeth can start to settle back. Wearing aligners 20 to 22 hours a day keeps movement steady so each new tray fits as planned.

What happens if you don't wear aligners enough?

If aligners are worn less than 20 to 22 hours a day, teeth fall behind the plan. The next tray may not fit, gaps appear between aligner and teeth, and treatment takes longer, often needing extra refinement aligners.

  • The next aligner feels very tight or does not seat fully.
  • Gaps appear between the aligner and teeth, called “non-tracking”.
  • Treatment takes longer, and extra refinement aligners may be needed.

Even when aligners are worn well, not every planned movement is achieved in one round;[1] poor wear makes this worse.

A sample day with aligners

TimeWhat you doAligner out
BreakfastRemove, eat, brush or rinse, replaceAbout 30 minutes
LunchRemove, eat, rinse, replaceAbout 30 minutes
Tea or snackPlain water with aligners in; tea or snacks with them outAbout 15–20 minutes
DinnerRemove, eat, brush, replaceAbout 30–40 minutes
NightSleep with aligners inNone

That adds up to around 2 hours out, well within the target.

Tips for wearing aligners in daily life

  • Group your eating: frequent snacking and sweet tea through the day are the commonest reasons for low wear time.
  • Carry a case: wrapping aligners in a napkin is how most get thrown away.
  • Set reminders: a phone timer after meals helps.
  • Travel and weddings: plan ahead; you can remove them briefly for a big event and put them back promptly.
  • Brush before replacing: food trapped under aligners raises the risk of decay.

Can you drink with aligners in?

You can drink plain water with aligners in. Remove them for tea, coffee, juice, milk and soft drinks, because sugar and acid get trapped against the teeth and hot drinks can warp the plastic.

Plain water, yes. Tea, coffee, juices and soft drinks should be taken with aligners out, because sugar and acid get trapped against the teeth and hot drinks can distort the plastic. See eating and drinking with aligners.

Frequently asked questions

Can I wear aligners only at night?

No. Night-only wear is for retainers after treatment, not active aligners.

Is 18 hours a day enough?

It is below the recommended 20 to 22 hours and can slow progress. Occasional short days happen, but regular low wear causes problems.

Should I sleep with aligners?

Yes. Night time is a large part of your daily wear.

What if I forget them for a day?

Put them back in as soon as you can and tell your orthodontist if the aligner no longer fits. You may need to stay on that tray longer.

Do aligners need to be removed for exercise?

Usually not. For contact sports, a mouthguard may be advised.
