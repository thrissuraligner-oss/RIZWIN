Patient information

What to expect at your first visit, and how to look after your teeth during and after treatment.

Dr. Rizwin Aligner Orthodontist, ThrissurPending clinical review by Dr. RizwinUpdated 29 September 2026

Before, during and after treatment

Your first visit

  • A conversation about your goals and concerns
  • Examination of teeth, gums, jaw joints and bite
  • Photographs, and X-rays where clinically indicated
  • Digital scan or impressions if needed
  • A clear explanation of options, timeline and cost

What to bring

  • Any previous dental records or X-rays
  • A list of medicines you take and any allergies
  • Questions you would like answered
  • For children: a parent or guardian

Aligner aftercare

  • Wear trays 20 to 22 hours a day
  • Remove to eat and drink anything but water
  • Brush before replacing trays
  • Keep trays in their case, never in a tissue
  • Bring all trays to review visits

Braces aftercare

  • Brush after meals and use interdental brushes
  • Avoid hard and sticky foods
  • Wear elastics exactly as instructed
  • Call us if a wire pokes or a bracket comes loose
  • Keep review appointments

Retainers: keeping your result

After treatment, teeth tend to drift back. Almost every patient needs some retention, and the best type and duration depend on your case. Follow the wear schedule and keep retainers safe and clean. Read why retainers matter.
