How it works

From your first video call to your final smile

An online consultation is a dedicated 30-minute video call with me. You send your details and smile photos, and if treatment is advised, your records are taken at a partner clinic near you, your 3D plan is reviewed online, and your aligners are made in my in-house unit.

  1. 1

    Online, video call

    Video consultation

    Send your details and smile photos. A dedicated video call is arranged at a time that suits you, and I address your concerns personally.

  2. 2

    Partner clinic

    Diagnostic records

    If treatment is advised, your diagnostic records (X-rays and a 3D scan) are planned and arranged at a partner clinic near you.

  3. 3

    Online

    3D treatment review

    Your plan is discussed with you properly: a 3D video of how your teeth will move and how your smile will look, how long treatment will take, and exactly what it will cost.

  4. 4

    Your decision

    You approve the plan

    Take your time. Ask questions, request changes, and approve only when you’re happy. The fee is in writing.

  5. 5

    Clinic

    Aligners delivered

    Once you approve, your aligners are made in my in-house unit and delivered to you at a clinic near you, within weeks.

  6. 6

    Online & clinic

    Proper monitoring

    Regular check-ins, online and in person, so every aligner fits and every tooth stays on track, all the way to your final smile.

Consultations worldwide

Wherever you are, you can consult with me here.

Living outside India? Planning a trip home or a visit to India? Book your online consultation from anywhere in the world. Calls are scheduled in your time zone.

  • Living abroad: Consult online and get an honest opinion on your smile, without travelling.
  • Visiting India: Plan before you fly, then do your scan and aligner fitting during your visit.
