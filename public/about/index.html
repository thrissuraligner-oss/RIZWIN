Meet Dr. Rizwin, orthodontist and clear aligner specialist in Thrissur

An orthodontist from Thrissur, Kerala. I’ve specialised in clear aligners, run my own in-house aligner unit, and I’m a certified Invisalign, ClearCorrect and Spark provider and aligner educator.

About me

I’m Dr. Rizwin. I’m an orthodontist, and aligners are my specialty.

I’m an orthodontist from Thrissur, Kerala. I completed my MDS in Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics at Yenepoya University, Mangalore, where my thesis studied different clear aligner materials. My research has won Best Paper awards at national orthodontic conferences.

I have advanced training in clear aligner therapy and myofunctional appliances. I treat all malocclusions, from crowding, spacing and open bites to the most complex cases, choosing fixed braces, lingual braces, expanders, functional or myofunctional appliances, or clear aligners according to what each case truly needs. Whatever your malocclusion, my goal never changes: a bite that works as beautifully as it looks, and a smile you’ll never want to hide.

Over the years, clear aligners became the work I love most. As a certified Invisalign, ClearCorrect and Spark provider, I could let software decide how your teeth should move, but software has never seen someone cover their mouth when they laugh. I have. That’s why I plan every case myself.

I run my own in-house aligner unit, so the aligners I prescribe are designed, printed, formed and checked under my supervision, from scan to final tray. I’m also an aligner educator: through The Aligner Project, I share what I’ve learned about clear aligners with fellow dentists. And I consult patients online, across India and abroad.

Chapter 1: The spark

I fell in love with a look in people’s eyes.

Long before aligners or labs, there was a moment I kept seeing: the day a patient’s treatment was finished and they looked in the mirror for the first time. Their smile changed, but what really changed was their eyes. There was a spark of confidence that hadn’t been there before.

That spark is why I chose orthodontics. Straight teeth are the result; a person who smiles without thinking twice is the reason.

A smile you can see. Confidence you can see in their eyes.

Chapter 2: The training

Learning why teeth move.

I did my MDS in Orthodontics & Dentofacial Orthopaedics at Yenepoya University, Mangalore. I wanted to understand the science behind every movement, not just the appliances, and my research went on to win Best Paper awards at national orthodontic conferences.

Even then, aligners had caught my attention. My thesis compared different clear aligner materials: how they behave, how they hold their force, and what makes one better than another. Without knowing it yet, I was laying the first brick of my own aligner unit.

Chapter 3: Every kind of bite

Braces, lingual, functional, and lessons from all of them.

With advanced training in myofunctional appliances, I treated every kind of case: fixed braces, lingual braces hidden behind the teeth, expanders, and functional and myofunctional appliances for growing children. It taught me the most important rule in orthodontics: the diagnosis matters more than the appliance.

Chapter 4: The turning point

Then aligners changed how I plan.

Clear aligners let me design every tooth’s movement in 3D before treatment even begins, and patients loved how discreet they were. I specialised in them and became a certified Invisalign, ClearCorrect and Spark provider.

But one thing kept bothering me. The plan was mine, yet the aligners came from an outside lab. Between my plan and my patient’s smile was a step I didn’t control.

Chapter 5: My own unit

So I built my own aligner unit.

So instead of relying on lab-made aligners, I decided to make them myself. I set up my own in-house aligner unit, so I’m in complete control of every step and can deliver the best aligners I possibly can.

Today every aligner I prescribe is designed, printed, formed and checked under my supervision, from the first scan to the final tray. If something needs adjusting, I don’t wait on anyone; it’s fixed down the corridor.

Chapter 6: Passing it on

Now I share what I’ve learned.

As an aligner educator, I help fellow dentists understand clear aligners better through The Aligner Project. And every week, I meet new patients online from across India and abroad.

Years later, with every tool from braces to my own aligner unit, every case still ends the same way: with that spark in someone’s eyes.

The next spark could be yours.
